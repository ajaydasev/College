Festivals of India

India is a land of diverse cultures and traditions. Festivals are an important part of Indian life and are celebrated with great joy and enthusiasm. Some major festivals of India include Diwali, Holi and Onam.

Diwali

Diwali, also known as the Festival of Lights, is one of the most widely celebrated festivals in India. People light lamps, decorate their homes and exchange sweets with family and friends.

Holi

Holi is the festival of colours. People celebrate it by applying coloured powders and water to one another. It is a festival of joy, friendship and togetherness.

Onam

Onam is a major festival celebrated in Kerala. It is associated with the legendary King Mahabali and is celebrated with flowers, traditional food, games and cultural activities.
