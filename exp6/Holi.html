Holi - Festival of Colours

History

Holi has ancient roots in India and is associated with several traditional stories. One of the most famous stories is that of Prahlada and Holika. The festival is also connected with Lord Krishna, especially in the Braj region of India.

Significance

Holi represents the victory of good over evil and is also associated with the arrival of spring. It is a time when people come together, forget differences and celebrate happiness and friendship.

How People Celebrate

People celebrate Holi by playing with coloured powders and coloured water. Families and friends meet each other and share sweets and traditional food. The celebration is often accompanied by music, dancing and gatherings.
