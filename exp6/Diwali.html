Diwali - Festival of Lights

History

Diwali has a long history and is celebrated in different ways across India. In the Hindu tradition, one of the well-known stories associated with Diwali is the return of Lord Rama to Ayodhya after defeating Ravana. People are said to have welcomed Rama by lighting rows of lamps.

Significance

Diwali symbolizes the victory of light over darkness and good over evil. It is also considered an important time for new beginnings, family gatherings and spreading happiness.

How People Celebrate

People clean and decorate their homes, light diyas and candles, wear new clothes and exchange sweets and gifts. Families gather together and many people also perform prayers. Fireworks are traditionally associated with the festival in many places.
